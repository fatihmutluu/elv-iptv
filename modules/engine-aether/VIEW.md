# `AetherSurface` view (`@elv/engine-aether`)

How `AetherSurfaceView` (`ios/Engine/AetherSurfaceView.swift`) presents an engine's video and subtitles on tvOS, iOS and iPadOS (player core spec 6.2). The module's contract and rules are in `AGENTS.md`.

## Surface & 9-Slot ASS Subtitles
- One `AetherPlayerView`, created with the `ExpoView` and never replaced, sits beneath an unfocusable `AetherSubtitleOverlayView` (`ios/Engine/AetherSubtitleOverlayView.swift`, `SubtitlePresentation.swift`, `Core/SubtitleLayout.swift`) and black `ShutterOverlayView`. All subviews set `isUserInteractionEnabled = false`, `isAccessibilityElement = false` and `canBecomeFocused == false`, so tvOS D-pad focus and React Native overlays drawn above the surface compose cleanly over the picture.
- On `.loopback` and `.software` routes, `AetherSubtitleOverlayView` buckets active `.text` and `.richText` `SubtitleCue`s (`cue.startTime <= clock.sourceTime < cue.endTime`, ignoring whitespace-only runs) into 9 lazy ASS `\an1`–`\an9` alignment slots (`nil` -> `\an2` bottom-center) positioned within `videoRect.intersection(bounds)` so `resizeMode="fill"` never clips text off-screen. Bitmap `.image` cues (`PGS`, `DVB`, `DVD`) map 1:1 onto `videoRect` for anamorphic SD/HD planes (`720x576`, `720x480`, `1440x1080`) while expanding 16:9 Blu-ray canvases onto cropped wide video; pooled `UIImageView`s are trimmed when empty. On `.remoteBypass` (HLS WebVTT), `AVPlayer` renders legible renditions natively.
- Content-aware `SubtitlePresentation` diffing detects `body` and `placement` mutations, refreshes `.loopback` `videoSize` when `AVPlayerItem` / `firstFrame` / surface binding settles, and clears cues immediately on `load`, `retry`, `stop`, `recreate`, `release`, `.ended`, error, unbind, surface loss, track switch and `selectSubtitleTrack(null)`.
- `AetherSurfaceView` implements `prepareForRecycle()` reset and uses `SurfaceCleanupState` in `deinit` to detach `IdleTimer` and unbind its `AetherPlayerView` synchronously on `@MainActor` by `ObjectIdentifier` without capturing `UIView` across isolation boundaries.
- Surface availability is `window != nil && !appInBackground && boundEngine != nil`, driven by `didMoveToWindow` and `UIApplication` background/foreground notifications to emit `surface` `created` and `destroyed`.

## Reports, Zap Shutter & Picture Shape
- `EngineMainDriver` binds at most one `AetherSurfaceView` per `EngineObject`. Rebinding unbinds any previous view (`surface` `destroyed`) before attaching `playerView` to `AetherBackend` (`engine.bind(view:)`) and pushing `core.viewSnapshot` (`shutterCovered`, `screenOn`). Every shutter, subtitle and screen-on update checks `boundEngine === engine`.
- `ShutterOverlayView` starts visible (`isHidden = false`) before any media loads and covers from each `load` until `AetherEngine.$hasFirstFrameReadyForDisplay` fires for that serial. Audio-only streams keep the black shutter covered; `retry`, `stop` and `recreate` preserve the current shutter state.
- `resizeMode="fit"` (default) sets `AetherEngine.videoGravity = .resizeAspect`; `resizeMode="fill"` sets `.resizeAspectFill` (cropped to `bounds` via `clipsToBounds = true`).

## Screen On & Background Lifecycle (`IdleTimer`)
- `EngineCore` holds keep-screen-on at `playing` and post-play `buffering`, clearing it at `paused`, `ended`, error, `stop`, `release` and surface loss. `IdleTimer` drives `UIApplication.shared.isIdleTimerDisabled` across active foreground owners, re-asserts it on `willEnterForegroundNotification` / `didBecomeActiveNotification`, and pairs with `AVPlayer.preventsDisplaySleepDuringVideoPlayback = true`.
- `AetherEngine.backgroundPlaybackEnabled` is `false` in `0.1`: entering the background marks the surface unavailable (`surface` `destroyed` -> `stopMedia()` -> `IdleTimer` off -> `error` `surface-lost`), parking `@elv/playback` in `awaitingSurface` without burning recovery rungs or holding a provider connection until foreground return emits `surface` `created`.
